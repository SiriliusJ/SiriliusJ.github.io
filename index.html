Yiyang Jiang 江易扬

Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering
Washington University in St. Louis

I am fortunate to work with Prof. Xudong Chen.

My research interests lie in the mathematical foundations of generative modeling, particularly the geometry of probability distributions. A question that has long fascinated me is how to meaningfully compare distributions of images, and how such comparisons can help us understand and improve generative models.

My work so far has focused on graphons and related graph problems, and generators of Lie algebras of vector fields.
