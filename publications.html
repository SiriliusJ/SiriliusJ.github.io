    Algebraic Vector Fields on S² are 2-generated

    Yiyang Jiang, Xudong Chen

  2. 2026-09-02Preprint

    Integrable Generators of Polynomial Vector Fields for Complex Classical Lie Groups

    Yiyang Jiang, Xudong Chen

    Three complex-complete polynomial fields generate the polynomial vector-field Lie algebra for SLₙ(ℂ) (n ≥ 2), SOₙ(ℂ) (n ≥ 5), and Sp₂ₙ(ℂ) (n ≥ 1).

    Integrable Generators of Algebraic Vector Fields for Real Connected Semi-simple Matrix Groups

    Yiyang Jiang, Xudong Chen

  4. 2026-03-28Preprint

    Largest 2-regular Subgraphs in complete S-partite Graphs

    Yiyang Jiang, Xudong Chen

    We study largest 2-regular subgraphs in complete S-partite graphs. We give an O(|V(S)|^3) algorithm based on the template graph S and connect the problem to controllability and stability in structural systems.
